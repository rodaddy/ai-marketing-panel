# workspace-template/

The starting shape of a panel workspace, copied out by `init-panel.ts` when a
panel is built. Holds `PANEL-BRIEF.md` and `panel.json` at the top, plus the
`calibration/`, `dossiers/`, `evidence/`, `inputs/`, `owner/`, and `runs/`
folders.

Templates only. Real panel content lives in the copied workspace, never here.
