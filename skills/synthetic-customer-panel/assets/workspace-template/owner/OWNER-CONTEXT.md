# Owner Context

This file feeds synthesis and rewriting only. Do not give it to panel seats.

## Organization or owner

State who owns the artifact.

## Offer or action

Describe what the audience is being asked to understand, believe, or do.

## Audience promise

State the truthful value and who it is for.

## Proof available

List substantiated proof, demonstrations, examples, and source locations.

## Claims and constraints

List forbidden, unverified, regulated, or easily misunderstood claims.

## Voice

Describe tone, vocabulary, cadence, and phrases to avoid. Match the voice without
turning it into parody.

## Call to action

State the desired action and the smallest acceptable next step.
