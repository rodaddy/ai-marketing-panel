# Source Notes

Record what each source supports. Do not paste secrets or unnecessary personal
data.

## Source entry template

- Source: <interview label, local document, analytics report, or URL>
- Date accessed or observed: <date>
- Source type: <first-party interview, customer language, analytics, primary
  source, authoritative secondary source, modeled assumption>
- Scope: <who, where, and what this evidence covers>
- Supports: <specific roster or dossier observations>
- Does not support: <limits and gaps>
- Reliability: <high, medium, or low with reason>
